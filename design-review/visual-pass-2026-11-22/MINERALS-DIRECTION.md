# Minerals — direction from the concept sheets

Source: `reference-art/minerals/<ore>/` (concept sheets, six variants each) and
`lineup/`. The game is low-poly: judge **silhouette, colour identity, facets and
glow** at gameplay size (an ore tile is ~40–60 px on screen), never surface detail.

| ore | silhouette | colour | facets | glow | the tell at gameplay size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Copper** | round, lumpy nodule; a honeycomb/cellular shell | verdigris teal-green crust | none on the shell; pitted cells | hot orange-red glows from *inside* the cells | teal crust + orange dots: the only two-tone "cratered" ore |
| **Iron** | blocky cube or a cluster of short dark prisms on a cube | gunmetal / blue-black, cold sparkle | hard flat faces, right angles | none, or tiny blue glints | the dark, square, non-glowing one — reads by shape and specular, not light |
| **Silver** | splayed spray of thin long needles fanning up out of rock | bright cool grey-white, metallic | long thin blades | almost none; bright specular edges | spiky radiating fan, pale and metallic, no colour |
| **Gold** | rough boulder split open, rock outside | warm yellow-gold to amber | broken plates/cracks | warm amber glow leaking from cracks/inside | rock-with-golden-wound; warm yellow, softer than ember |
| **Ruby** | chunky stubby hexagonal crystals, 1–3 on rock | deep crimson to hot pink | big clean facets | strong pink-red inner glow, light pooling at base | saturated red-pink, chunky gem facets |
| **Voidopal** | smooth egg/ovoid, faceted all over, on a dark base | iridescent blue–violet–magenta with flashes | many small facets | inner multicolour glow, brightest at base | rounded egg + shifting violet/blue rainbow |
| **Diamond** | single clean geometric gem (octahedron / brilliant / pointed prism) | colourless to pale ice-blue | crisp large facets, sharp | sparkle + light caustics, soft cool glow | the cleanest geometric silhouette; clear/white, hard edges |
| **Embershard** | flat, jagged, broken black shards/slabs | obsidian black-blue | sharp fractured planes | orange sparks/flecks + glowing seams in the cracks | black shard peppered with ember sparks; darker than gold, angrier than copper |
| **Frostbloom** (CRYOS) | starburst / sea-urchin spray of crystals all directions | pale periwinkle / lavender-white | many thin points | soft cold white glow from centre | a radial frost flower — round spiky ball, lavender |
| **Rime Salt** (CRYOS native) | cube / cubic block, frosted top | glacier blue, translucent | blocky internal fractures | soft cyan inner light | a translucent blue *cube* with frost — iron's shape, ice's colour and glow |
| **Nacre** (MAELIS native) | scalloped shell / fan, layered ridges, on slate | pearl white with pink-teal-violet iridescence | ribbed layers, not facets | soft pearly sheen, not a hard glow | ribbed shell + rainbow pearl sheen |
| **Veinlight** (fungus, fuel) | branching coral / fern / feathered fronds, or teal crystal cluster | bioluminescent teal-cyan | soft fronds | strong teal glow lighting the base rock | organic branching teal glow — the only "plant" |
| **Mimic** | voidopal-like faceted ovoid, but in a cracked blue-grey *rock* husk | blue/violet facets | facets inside a split husk | **red-orange glowing crack lines** across the husk | looks like a premium gem, but the red veins/cracks give it away (keep current in-game tell or an equally fair one) |

## Distinctness pairs to watch

- **Gold vs Embershard vs Copper** (all warm glow): gold = yellow, broad warm wound; embershard = black shard with pinpoint sparks; copper = teal shell with orange cells.
- **Silver vs Diamond vs Frostbloom vs Rime Salt** (all pale): silver = fan of needles, grey metal; diamond = one crisp gem; frostbloom = round lavender starburst; rime salt = translucent blue cube.
- **Voidopal vs Mimic**: same family by design; the mimic must carry the red-crack tell.
- **Iron vs rock**: iron does not glow, so it must separate from host rock by its gunmetal-blue colour, crisp cubic facets and specular.
- **Ruby vs everything**: the only saturated red-pink.
- **Every ore vs its host rock**: the reference rock is always matte dark grey; the ore sits on or erupts from it.
