# Round 1 plan

Best = r0. Scores: ui 6.1, characters 4.6, lighting 4.4, identity 4.4, vaults 4.3, rock 4.2, fluids 3.8, minerals 3.4.
Issues raised by several reviewers: emissives don't glow or light what's around them (lighting, rock, fluids, identity, minerals, characters); world hue collapses at mid depth and CRYOS/MAELIS deep drift into each other's hues (rock, identity, fluids, lighting); pools are flat opaque blocks (rock, fluids, identity, lighting).

Fixes, run sequentially (they share rendering code), each its own commit:

1. **F1 minerals — ore identity rework** (targets: minerals). Per MINERALS-DIRECTION: copper teal crust + glowing orange cells; iron dark gunmetal with blue specular, no glow; silver denser needle fan; gold glowing warm wound in rock; embershard black shard with orange seams; rime salt translucent blue cube with cyan glow; nacre ribbed pearly fan; diamond pale/colourless with sparkle; voidopal per-face blue-violet-magenta; mimic: red-orange crack tell; ore tile reads as host rock, not a black square. Files: src/world/gemshapes.ts, gem material in src/world/chunks.ts, ore host shade in src/world/tiles.ts.
2. **F2 fluids — glowing pools that light their stone** (targets: fluids). Emissive fluid material with a brighter surface/meniscus band and smooth pulse; per-world fluid hue (VEIL molten orange, CRYOS cyan, MAELIS sea-green brine); fluid colour spills onto nearby rock faces via a per-instance glow term baked at chunk build (no new lights). Same spill mechanism for heat fissures so they light their stone. Files: src/world/chunks.ts, src/world/worlds.ts (fluid colours), pulse in src/main.ts.
3. **F3 identity — one hue per world at every depth** (targets: identity; also rock). Retune worlds.ts rock band colours and fogStops amb/fog so each world keeps its hue family through every band (VEIL amber/rust, CRYOS ice-blue/cyan, MAELIS sea-green/teal), varying value/saturation not hue; plus a faint per-world strata emissive accent in the rock so the deep bands carry hue without raising ambient. Files: src/world/worlds.ts, rock material in src/world/chunks.ts. (Core colours left alone this round: the CRYOS Heart's green is written into its ending text — logged for the user.)
4. **F4 vaults — a cool hazy masonry backdrop and warm sconce pools** (targets: vaults). A dim, cooler back-wall layer of dressed stone behind the playfield (value above black, below footing), black kept for pits/void; sconces/lanterns get a warm pool on nearby masonry; fang strips get a hot emissive edge. Files: src/game/vault.ts, src/game/vaultkit.ts (visual parts only).
5. **F5 lighting — bloom on true emissives** (targets: lighting). EffectComposer + half-res UnrealBloomPass with a high threshold, OutputPass for tone mapping, for the mine scene and the vault scene; a "Bloom" toggle in the settings panel (default on). Files: new src/fx/post.ts, src/main.ts render calls, src/game/settings.ts, the settings panel in src/ui/panels.ts, vault render hook.
